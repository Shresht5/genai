Interstellar is a 2014 science-fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan. The movie stars Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Jessica Chastain, and Michael Caine. It follows former NASA pilot Cooper, who joins a mission through a wormhole near Saturn to search for a new habitable planet for humanity. The film explores space travel, time dilation, gravity, love, and humanity's survival. Interstellar received an IMDb rating of approximately 8.7 out of 10 and is widely regarded as one of the most popular science-fiction films of its era.
